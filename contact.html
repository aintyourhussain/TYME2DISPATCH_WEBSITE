LET’S START WITH THE BASICS.

Make your initial conversation more useful with a few details about your operation.

01

What to include in your inquiry

Share your truck type, preferred lanes or operating regions, preferred billing plan, and the best way to reach you. You do not need to send private identification documents through the initial contact form.

02

What happens next

Once an inquiry reaches our connected inbox or form service, the team can review your carrier requirements and discuss availability, onboarding steps, and dispatch terms. Submission is not a dispatch contract.

03

Choosing your plan

You can ask about the percentage rates or choose the $300-per-week flat rate for any of the three equipment types. No percentage surcharge applies to the weekly flat-rate plan.
