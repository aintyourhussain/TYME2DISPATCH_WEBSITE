EACH STEP, EXPLAINED.

Know what to expect before making commitments or sharing operating preferences.

01

Before your first load

We discuss your equipment type, carrier authority, service area, preferred operating schedule, and dispatch fee option. We also clarify communication channels and who has authority to approve freight.

02

When a potential load comes up

Your dispatcher reviews the information available, discusses rate and route considerations, and shares relevant details. Carrier approval comes before finalizing commitments.

03

During transit and after delivery

We help coordinate broker communication and paperwork as agreed. The carrier remains responsible for safe operation, regulatory compliance, and accurate delivery documentation.

04

Getting the right fit

Some carriers prioritize regional lanes or home time; others want longer trips. Sharing priorities early helps the team search and coordinate more effectively. No specific earnings or weekly load count is guaranteed.
