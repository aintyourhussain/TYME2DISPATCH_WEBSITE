PRIVACY NOTICE.

This is a draft privacy notice for the website owner to review and customize before public launch.

Information collected

When you submit the carrier inquiry form, TYME2DISPATCH may receive the name, email address, phone number, equipment category, pricing preference, operating region, and message you provide.

How information is used

Inquiry information is intended to respond to requests for dispatch support, discuss services, and communicate about a potential business relationship.

Website hosting and forms

The static website can run on different hosting platforms. Once the owner connects a form endpoint, the selected form service or backend may process and store submitted inquiries. The owner must document the selected provider, who can access the data, its retention policy, and applicable privacy notices before enabling the form.

Data sharing

Information may be processed by the website host and form provider to deliver the requested services. Do not submit highly sensitive personal or financial information through the form.

Contact and updates

Company contact information and the date this policy takes effect must be added by the site owner before launch. Privacy practices should be reviewed if analytics, advertising, chat, or additional providers are later enabled.
