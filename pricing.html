GOOD TO KNOW

PRICING FAQS.

Is the $300 weekly plan charged in addition to the percentage fee?

No. The $300 weekly plan is an alternative to percentage pricing. Customers on the weekly plan pay only $300 per week in dispatch fees.

Does the $300 weekly plan apply to every equipment type?

Yes. The flat weekly plan is available for semi trucks, hotshots, and box trucks.

Can I choose percentage pricing instead?

Yes. Semi trucks are 5%, hotshots are 7%, and box trucks are 10% under the percentage-based option.

Before you choose

KNOW WHAT YOU PAY.

A practical explanation of both fee structures and what to confirm in your written agreement.

01

Understanding percentage-based pricing

The dispatch fee is calculated as a percentage of the agreed load revenue, subject to the contract terms: 5% for semi trucks, 7% for hotshots, and 10% for box trucks. Confirm the fee basis and billing schedule during onboarding.

02

Understanding the weekly flat rate

The alternative is $300 per week for full dispatch service, regardless of whether you operate a semi, hotshot, or box truck. If you select this plan, no 5%, 7%, or 10% dispatch percentage is added.

03

Which plan makes sense?

A percentage plan varies with load revenue, while the flat rate is predictable. Use the calculator to compare illustrative fees for a given week, then review your operating needs before making a choice.

04

Billing and onboarding questions

Ask about when the weekly period starts, when invoices are issued, termination and pause policies, and any necessary carrier paperwork. These details should be confirmed in a written dispatch agreement; this page is not a contract.
